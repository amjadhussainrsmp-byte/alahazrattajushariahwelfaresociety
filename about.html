Our story

Rooted in service. Focused on people.

The Ala Hazrat Tajushshariah Welfare Society was established to translate concern for people into practical, community-centred action. Founded by Dr Farmaan Hasan Khan, the Society has been associated with initiatives that connect people facing hardship with educational support, medical assistance and opportunities to build skills.

Its approach recognises that a person's circumstances should not decide whether they can access learning, treatment or the chance to shape a better future. By working with volunteers, educators, healthcare professionals and community partners, the Society aims to help support reach people who need it.

“The purpose of welfare is not simply to offer help today, but to make tomorrow more hopeful.”

From education support to health-focused drives and environmental action, the Society's work reflects a simple principle: compassion matters most when it becomes something people can feel in their everyday lives.

What guides us

Our values, put into practice.

We believe meaningful community work should be compassionate, inclusive, responsible and focused on lasting opportunity.

🤝

Human dignity

Every person deserves to be treated with respect. Support should protect dignity and respond to real needs without discrimination.

📚

Education and opportunity

Learning can open doors. We support efforts that help students from financially constrained backgrounds continue their education.

🩺

Health and well-being

We encourage collaboration that can make medical guidance, treatment pathways and health-related support more accessible.

🌿

Responsibility for the planet

Tree-planting and environmental awareness initiatives encourage people to care for the places and communities they share.

🌍

Harmony and inclusion

We value cooperation across communities and believe service can build understanding, trust and a stronger sense of shared responsibility.

🧭

Accountability

Trust is essential to non-profit work. We aim to communicate clearly, collaborate responsibly and keep the needs of communities at the centre.

Our mission

Make support more accessible. Make opportunity more real.

Our mission is to work with communities and partners to help people facing social and economic barriers access support, develop skills and move towards a more secure future.

We seek to turn goodwill into practical action—whether that means helping a student continue learning, connecting a patient with support, or encouraging local people to protect their environment.

The vision behind the work

Dr Farmaan Hasan Khan

Dr Farmaan Hasan Khan is the founder of Ala Hazrat Tajushshariah Welfare Society. Public reporting and his biographical profile associate his work with welfare initiatives in Bareilly, including healthcare support, educational coaching, computer learning and environmental drives.

The Society's work reflects a people-first vision: to encourage service, widen access to opportunity and bring community members together around constructive action. Its initiatives depend on cooperation among volunteers, medical professionals, educators and other supporters.
