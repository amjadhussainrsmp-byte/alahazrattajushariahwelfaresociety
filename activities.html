What we do

Learning, participation and community progress.

Our activities focus on practical knowledge, employable skills, social awareness and shared responsibility. Through workshops, campaigns and community programmes, we aim to make opportunities and useful information more accessible.

Our key initiatives

Activities & programmes

Explore the different areas of our work.

01
Skill development

UPSDM Project

AHT Skills Welfare Society states that it is associated with the Uttar Pradesh Skill Development Mission (UPSDM) as an approved training partner, supporting workforce-oriented training and skill development in Uttar Pradesh.

Visit UPSDM →
02
Training & livelihood

Skill Training Workshops

Practical training areas include tailoring, computer literacy, digital marketing, handicrafts, beauty and wellness, and entrepreneurship—helping participants develop useful, livelihood-oriented capabilities.

03
Public awareness

Awareness Campaigns

Awareness themes include road safety, women’s rights, child education, health and hygiene, digital awareness and drug de-addiction—encouraging informed choices and responsible citizenship.

04
Community service

Community Engagement

Community programmes include festival participation, community clean-up drives and charitable distributions. Support initiatives may include blankets, ration kits and school supplies for people and families in need.

05
Arts & expression

Performing Arts & Talent Shows

Musical performances, dance, painting and poetry recitals offer participants a platform to express creativity, share their talents and engage with wider audiences.

06
Digital inclusion

Digital Literacy & Online Learning

Mobile classes, webinars and e-learning introduce computer skills, internet safety and practical digital tools for learning and everyday life.
