Who we are

Turning compassion into meaningful action.

Ala Hazrat Tajushshariah Welfare Society is a non-profit organisation based in Bareilly, Uttar Pradesh. Founded by Dr Farmaan Hasan Khan, the Society works to connect compassion with practical support—helping underserved people access essential services and new opportunities.

Our approach is rooted in a simple belief: service should reach people where it matters most. From healthcare and educational support to skill-building and environmental initiatives, we work alongside volunteers, professionals and community partners to create positive change.
